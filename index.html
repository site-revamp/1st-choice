About First Choice

Pest management that starts with understanding the problem.

First Choice Pest Specialist provides pest-management services for residential, commercial and industrial environments in Singapore. Its approach focuses on identifying pest activity and selecting suitable treatment and prevention measures for the property.

Rather than presenting every customer with the same solution, the company describes its service as customised to the site, pest concern and operating environment.

A considered approach to pest control

Inspection, targeted treatment and practical prevention advice help customers understand the issue and the recommended next steps.
