# First Choice Pest Specialist — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

First Choice Pest Specialist Pte. Ltd. provides customised pest-management services for residential, commercial, industrial and food-service environments in Singapore. Its approach combines inspection, treatment recommendations, targeted pest management and prevention advice.

The proposed homepage helps homeowners, property managers and business operators identify relevant services while making the company's specialist capabilities easier to evaluate.

## Main Improvements

1. **Accurate homepage statistics**  
   Remove the zero-value experience, client and extermination-rate counters unless verified replacements are available. Clearly label any substantiated figures to address the unfinished statistics that currently weaken credibility.

2. **A clearer customer journey**  
   Consolidate repeated service descriptions, pest categories, trust statements and calls to action. Structure the page around property type, pest concern, treatment approach and enquiry so visitors can reach relevant information with less repetition.

3. **More prominent specialist capabilities**  
   Give the promoted cockroach gel bait solution, food-service expertise, customised treatment process and aftercare clearer visual presentation. Use authentic photographs, concise explanations and relevant enquiry links, with product and aftercare claims limited to confirmed details.

## Design Improvements

- Establish a clearer hierarchy between the opening proposition, service selection, treatment process and supporting evidence.
- Use consistent section headings and layouts to reduce repeated content and improve scanning.
- Place relevant enquiry actions beside the services and specialist information they support.

## Implementation Status

This summary documents the proposed redesign direction. Implementation has not been verified against a redesigned homepage draft, and replacement statistics require substantiation.
